# BreatheRoute

> **One-line pitch**: A bilingual web app that helps families along the Highway 101 corridor, especially those with kids who have asthma or allergies, see how close their school or home is to freeway traffic pollution and what they can do about it.

## Target Area & Audience
- **District**: CA-16 (Palo Alto, East Palo Alto, Mountain View, parts of San Jose)
- **Primary Users**: Parents and school staff in freeway-adjacent communities with few air monitors.

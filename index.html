Congressional App Challenge 2026 - Dev Log & Specs

Target District & Positioning

District: CA-16 (Palo Alto, East Palo Alto, Mountain View, San Jose)

Pitch: BreatheRoute is a bilingual web app helping families along Highway 101 see how close their school or home is to freeway pollution and access official health guidance.

Technical Architecture & Stack

  • Geospatial Calculations: GeoPandas / Turf.js projecting line-to-point buffer calculations (meter-based metric buffers converted to 500ft threshold).
  • Data Sources: Caltrans GIS US-101 highway vector geometries & California Department of Education (CDE) Public Schools Dataset.
  • Deployment: Streamlit Community Cloud (Python/GeoPandas production deployment repository) & Static web prototype preview.

Technical Challenge & Solution

Challenge: Inaccurate distance measuring using spherical Lat/Lng coordinates causing distortion around highway curves.

Solution: Projected vector calculations using EPSG:3310 (California Albers) / Turf distance geodesics to compute exact perpendicular line distance to US-101 line segments.
